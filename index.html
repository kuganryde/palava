    PALAVA

    Rise of an Ancient Kingdom

      • Select: click a unit or drag a box (Shift adds).
      • Move: right-click ground. Pick a formation in the bottom panel.
      • Build: choose a building (1–5), click a green spot; Esc / right-click cancels.
      • Camera: WASD / arrows pan, Q / E rotate, wheel or +/− zoom (zoom in far for an inspection view), middle-drag pans, H centres on the palace. M mutes sound.
      • Fight: right-click an enemy to attack it; F then click for attack-move; Alt+1–9 saves a control group, Shift+1–9 recalls it (twice centres the camera). Destroy the red stronghold to win; keep your palace standing.
      • Recruit: select a finished Barracks, then Recruit.
      • Other: P pause, Esc menu, F3 performance stats.
